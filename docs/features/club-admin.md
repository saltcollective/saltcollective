## Summary

Club Admin is the management interface for Club Administrators. It provides everything a club needs to set up, maintain, and monitor their Business Hub - from first-run setup through to day-to-day business management and analytics.

## Problem Statement

Volunteer club administrators have limited time and limited technical ability. They need a management interface that is simple enough to use on a phone between other commitments, and powerful enough to keep the hub accurate and useful over time.

## Goals

- Give Club Admins full control over their hub and its businesses through a simple, low-friction interface
- Minimise the ongoing effort required to keep the hub maintained
- Surface enough data to help clubs demonstrate value to their business supporters

## Non-Goals

- Club Admin is not a general club management tool - it is scoped to the Business Hub only
- Club Admin does not handle financial transactions or invoicing between club and business
