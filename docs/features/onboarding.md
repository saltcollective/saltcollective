## Summary

Onboarding covers the end-to-end journey from a new club discovering Salt Collective to having a live, published hub. It spans account creation, subscription, first-run setup, and the deliberate go-live step.

## Problem Statement

A club that has agreed to subscribe needs a clear, low-friction path to a working hub. Any unnecessary friction at this stage risks churn before the product has delivered any value.

## Goals

- Get a new club from sign-up to a published hub with as little friction as possible
- Make the subscription commitment clear and straightforward before setup begins
- Ensure publishing is a deliberate, confident action rather than something that happens by accident

## Non-Goals

- Onboarding does not cover ongoing hub management - that is Club Admin
- Onboarding does not handle the sale or commercial negotiation before sign-up

## User Stories

| As a... | I want to... | So that... |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| Club Admin | Create an account and subscribe quickly | I can get started without a lengthy sales process |
| Club Admin | Publish my hub when I am ready | My hub goes live as a deliberate action, not accidentally |

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A new club can sign up, subscribe, and reach the admin area without contacting Salt Collective
- [ ] Publishing a hub is a separate, explicit action after setup is complete
